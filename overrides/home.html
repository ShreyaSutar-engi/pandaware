our mission

Prosthetic hands are expensive, and the engineering behind them is often locked away. PandaWare exists to change that: accessible, open-source assistive technology that anyone can build, inspect, and adapt — and a clear enough explanation of why it works that other engineers, makers, and amputees can build their own, whether that means printing this exact design or using it as a launchpad for something better.
